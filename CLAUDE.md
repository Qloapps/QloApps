Introduction (from the RFP)

Ottawa County Parks and Recreation Commission is seeking proposals from qualified vendors to provide a Campground Reservation and Management Software System. The system will be used to manage reservations, point of sale transactions, reporting, and administrative functions across multiple campgrounds and facilities operated by the County. The selected vendor will be responsible for providing a software solution that is easy to use, secure, and capable of meeting the County’s operational and financial needs. The vendor will also provide setup, training, migration, and ongoing support services to ensure successful implementation and operation of the system

A. Minimum Requirements

Ease of Use

Grid and Map view for easy reservation creation and modification, internal and external

Software that is desktop and mobile device optimized (responsive design or app-based)

The ability to check out as a guest and not require users to create an account with a password

Automated system that allows customers to cancel reservations on their own

Ability to manage more than one campground

Software meets or exceeds current or future ADA requirements

Reporting Capability

Ability to send automated communication to staff and customers (email, SMS, Microsoft Teams, or other supported channels)

Ability to run occupancy reports for custom date ranges and facility types while filtering out holds and internal reservations

Ability to show real-time sales and run end-of-day sales reports at an individual clerk level

Real-time inventory control and reporting capability

Ability to run an arrival and departure report for adjustable date ranges

Ability to track and control inventory

Built-in reporting and analytics tools, including customizable dashboards by the user role

Export capabilities to CSV, Excel, and PDF

Financial Capability

Ability to operate on an accrual-based accounting system

Ability to collect and account for sales tax and CVB assessments for individual site types and POS items

Point of Sale system that can accept cash, check, and card payments

Ability of the credit card processing system to work directly in the system with or without a physical terminal

A system that can integrate with credit card processing companies that meet PCI Compliance and can integrate payments with Huntington National Bank

The ability to use dynamic pricing and allow for custom pricing ranges

The ability to charge various rental prices based on day of the week, time of the year, and predetermined holidays

The ability to automatically recalculate charges when reservation adjustments are made by staff or customers

Ability to not require customers to pay a site lock fee to pick a specific site
